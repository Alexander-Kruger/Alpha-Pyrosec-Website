Core Engineering Pillars

Industrial-grade turnkey delivery designed to safeguard high-consequence capital infrastructure without disruption to operational output.

PILLAR 01

Critical Asset Gas Suppression

Total-flooding engineered gaseous extinguishing systems specifically designed to suppress class A, B, and C hazards within energized spaces without water damage.

Inert Gas (IG-55 / Pyroshield), CO2 High/Low Pressure, Novec 1230 / FK-5-1-12, FM-200 Clean Agents.

PILLAR 02

Industrial Water & Foam Deluge

Robust hydraulic suppression systems engineered for aggressive industrial environments, heavy processing installations, and high fire-load yards.

CORE TECHNOLOGIES

Automatic sprinkler lines, high-expansion foam-water deluge, ring hydrant loops, booster couplings, MV/HV spray nozzles.

DEPLOYMENT ENVIRONMENTS

Conveyor transfer towers, mineral thickener plants, chemical warehousing, bulk fuel offloading depots.

Intelligent Early Fire Detection

Ultra-high sensitivity monitoring capable of identifying pre-combustion off-gassing long before visible flame or thermal spikes develop.

CORE TECHNOLOGIES

VESDA LaserFOCUS air sampling, multi-sensor optical & rate-of-rise thermal heads, triple IR flame scanners, C-Tec control panels.

DEPLOYMENT ENVIRONMENTS

High-airflow data halls, mining electrical control centers (MCC), subterranean cable tunnels, pharmaceutical laboratories.

DEPLOYMENT ENVIRONMENTS

Multi-story corporate estates, manufacturing campuses, logistics distribution centers, heavy workshops.
